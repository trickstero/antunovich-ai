Evaluate subconsultant options and recommend team assembly for: $ARGUMENTS

Reference the procurement skill for the approved vendor list and past project pairings. Cross-reference with practice area requirements from CLAUDE.md.

## Deliverable Structure

**RECOMMENDATION:** Recommended subconsultant team (1–2 sentences with rationale).

**SCOPE REQUIREMENTS:**
- Disciplines needed (structural, MEP, civil, landscape, lighting, AV, security, vertical transport, envelope, historic preservation, sustainability/LEED, code, spec)
- Special expertise required (e.g., mass timber, curtain wall, adaptive reuse, WELL certification)
- Permit jurisdiction and local-presence requirements

**SUBCONSULTANT EVALUATION:**
- For each discipline, list 2–3 qualified firms from the approved vendor list
- Note past Antunovich project pairings and performance
- Flag any firms with current capacity issues or conflicts
- Include fee range benchmarks (% of construction cost by discipline)

**TEAM CHEMISTRY FACTORS:**
- Prior teaming history (which firms have worked together successfully)
- Client preferences or requirements (some owners mandate specific engineers)
- Geographic coverage (local office vs. remote for CA phase)
- Insurance and bonding requirements

**PURSUIT STRATEGY:**
- Recommended team for shortlist presentation
- Key differentiators this team brings vs. likely competitors
- Teaming agreement considerations (exclusive vs. non-exclusive)

**BUDGET IMPLICATIONS:**
- Estimated subconsultant fee as % of Antunovich fee
- Pass-through vs. marked-up structure recommendation
- Reimbursable scope items to negotiate

If this is for an RFP response, also include a draft team org chart description and relevant project experience pairings (Antunovich project + sub's complementary project).
