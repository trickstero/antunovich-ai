# SHPO Narrative Skill

## Secretary of the Interior's Standards
1. Property shall be used as historically
2. Historic character retained and preserved
3. Property recognized as physical record of its time
4. Changes with acquired historic significance retained
5. Distinctive materials/features/finishes preserved
6. Deteriorated features repaired, not replaced
7. No chemical/physical treatments causing damage
8. Archeological resources protected
9. New additions shall not destroy historic materials
